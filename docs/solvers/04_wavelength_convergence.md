# Wavelength Convergence

> Multi-wavelength signal processing for convergence detection.

## Overview

Wavelength Convergence processes overlapping frequency signals and identifies points of constructive and destructive interference. It is the framework's primary mechanism for detecting signal patterns across multiple sources.

## Method

1. **Wave decomposition** — Break each signal into its component frequencies
2. **Band alignment** — Align components to the 72-band spectrum
3. **Interference calculation** — Compute constructive/destructive interference patterns
4. **Convergence detection** — Identify points where multiple bands align

## Application

Used for pattern matching across independent sources, cross-validation of frequency detections, and identifying the base carrier frequency from partial observations.
