# The Three Tribes

> The three fundamental operations: C (decompose), X (intersect), Z (cancel).

## Overview

The Three Tribes represent the three fundamental operations of the 72-band framework. Every processing step is a combination of these three operations applied at specific band positions.

## The Operations

### C — The Decomposer
Structural analysis. Break a problem into its constituent frequencies. The C operation identifies the band positions of each component.

### X — The Intersector
State intersection. Find where two or more frequency states overlap. The X operation identifies common patterns across independent signals.

### Z — The Canceller
Nullification. Remove noise and irrelevant frequencies. The Z operation isolates the signal from the environment.

## Composition

Operations compose as: `Ω = {C, X, Z} × f`

Where f is the frequency band, and the cross-product generates all possible operation-band combinations.
