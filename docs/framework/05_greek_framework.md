## Introduction

The Greek expression of the framework is the most logically explicit. Where other cultures encode the pipeline as mythology, ritual, mathematics, or binary states, the Greek framework encodes it as **philosophy** — pure reasoning about the nature of structure, change, and observation. The Greek contribution to the framework is not a set of stories but a method: systematic inquiry delivered through question and answer.

The Greek framework is also the bridge between the ancient encodings and modern computational thinking. Pythagoras's harmonic ratios (2:1, 3:2, 4:3) directly encode the band spacing. Aristotle's logic formalizes the complement operation. The Socratic method is an assessor system — each question tests one dimension of a claim's integrity.

The generational succession (Kronos → Zeus) encodes the operational sequence: complement precedes cancel.

## 6. Forty-Two Assessors

The 42 are implicit in the Greek philosophical tradition:

1. **Socratic method:** Each question tests one dimension of a claim's integrity. The elenchus (cross-examination) is the assessment protocol. A claim that survives the questions converges; a claim that contradicts itself returns to the speaker for revision.

2. **Aristotle's 10 categories + 4 causes + 28 rhetorical topoi = 42 modes of inquiry.** Each mode tests a specific dimension of signal integrity — substance, quantity, quality, relation, place, time, position, state, action, affection (the categories); material, formal, efficient, final (the causes); and the 28 topoi as the assessor-by-assessor list.

3. **The Delphic maxims:** 147 surviving sayings, traditionally grouped as 42 primary and 105 secondary. "Know thyself" — the observer check. "Nothing in excess" — the band balance check.

The Greek assessors are the most philosophical of any framework — they test the logical consistency of the claim rather than its ritual purity or mathematical correctness.

## 8. Full Phase — Ekpyrosis

The Stoic conflagration. The universe periodically consumed by fire — the Z-Cancel applied globally. Everything returns to the primordial fire (pyr). Then the cycle begins again (palingenesis).

Heraclitus, fragment 30:
> "This world, which is the same for all, no one of gods or men has made. But it always was, is, and will be: an ever-living fire, with measures of it kindling and measures going out."

The "measures" are the bands. The "kindling" is the C operation. The "going out" is the Z operation. The "ever-living fire" is the X-the intersection that persists across cycles.

Ekpyrosis is unique among FULL phases in its **materiality**: where other frameworks describe a judgment, a dissolution, a return of light, or a destruction, Ekpyrosis describes a transformation of state — everything becomes fire, fire becomes everything. The operation does not judge or end. It converts.
