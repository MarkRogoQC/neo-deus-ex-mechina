## Introduction

The Chinese expression of the framework is the most compressively elegant. Where other cultures encode the 72 bands as named deities or numbered lists, the Chinese framework reduces the entire pipeline to a **binary frequency register**: the I Ching's 64 hexagrams (each a 6-bit state configuration) and the 8 trigrams (each a 3-bit base pattern). Together: 64 + 8 = 72 encoding units — mapping exactly to the 72-band spectrum.

The Chinese framework is unique in having survived **intact**. Jesuit missionaries attempted absorption in the 16th-17th centuries (Matteo Ricci's syncretism, the Chinese Rites Controversy), but Chinese scholars were more literate than the missionaries. The I Ching and Tao Te Ching were too old and too central to burn.

## 1. Three Operations

| Operation | Name | Symbol | Function |
|-----------|------|--------|----------|
| **C** (Complement) | Yáng (陽) | ⚊ (solid line) | Structural assertion. Light. Heat. The active principle. |
| **X** (Cross) | Dào (道) | — (the unnameable) | The intersection itself. The path between yáng and yīn. |
| **Z** (Cancel) | Yīn (陰) | ⚋ (broken line) | Nullification. Dark. Cold. The return to stillness. |

The yáng/yīn pair forms the binary encoding: a solid line (yáng) asserts presence (C), a broken line (yīn) cancels presence (Z). The Dào is the space between the lines — the intersection that makes the pattern visible.

**⚠️ Note on feminine obfuscation:** In every other framework, the feminine principle maps to C (complement/receptivity/wisdom) or X (connection/cross). Here, yīn (feminine) is mapped to Z — nullification, the operation that appears destructive. This is the Christian absorption pattern: the feminine principle was pushed to the role that seems negative. The original encoding likely had different assignments.

## 5. Encoding System

The I Ching — 64 hexagrams, each a 6-bit frequency register. 8 trigrams (bāguà) form the encoding alphabet. 2⁶ = 64 possible frequency states. Every hexagram is a named state configuration with a corrective instruction (the Judgment).

The I Ching does not predict the future. It **measures the inharmony of the present**. The hexagram that arises from the yarrow-stalk casting describes the current state configuration. The changing lines describe the direction of inharmony. The judgment describes the corrective operation.

The 64 hexagrams reduce to 8 trigrams. The 8 trigrams reduce to 3 lines (C, Z, and the space-between-that-is-X). The three lines reduce to the two principles (yáng and yīn) and the Dào that relates them.

64 hexagrams + 8 trigrams = 72. The 8 trigrams are the foundation patterns — the ONE bands of the 72-band spectrum. Each hexagram is two stacked trigrams: top three lines shape the output, bottom three lines shape the input. The hexagram IS the relationship between one 3-bit pattern and another.

## 6. Forty-Two Assessors

The 42 are encoded in three forms:

1. **Confucian scholarship:** 42 virtues catalogued — each a dimension of signal integrity in the moral register.

2. **T'ien T'ai Buddhist path:** 42 stages of the bodhisattva's progress — the assessor sequence as spiritual development.

3. **Tao Te Ching:** 81 chapters, variously grouped. The 42 appears consistently across Chinese traditions as a count of virtues, stages, and stations of spiritual progress.

Where the Egyptian assessors are a named list and the Vedic assessors are a performed ritual, the Chinese assessors are internal virtues — internalized integrity checks that the practitioner applies to their own state.

## 8. Full Phase — The Return

Hexagram 24: **Fù (復)** — Return. The winter solstice. One yáng line at the bottom of five yīn lines — the seed of light returning in deepest darkness.

> "Going and coming, the return. After seven days comes the return."

The FULL phase in the Chinese framework is not destruction (Ragnarök), not judgment (Weighing), not dissolution (Pralaya). It is **the return of light**. The one yáng line rising from five yīn — the complement emerging from complete cancel.

Lǎo Zǐ chapter 42:
> "The Dào gave birth to One. One gave birth to Two. Two gave birth to Three. Three gave birth to the ten thousand things."

This is the initialization sequence. The Dào (X), the One (C), the Two (yáng/yīn, C/Z), the Three (the first trigram), the ten thousand things (all 64 hexagrams). The FULL phase reverses the sequence: ten thousand things → three → two → one → Dào → unmanifest. Then the cycle restarts.
