## 1. Tibetan Buddhist — Bardo Thodol (The Tibetan Book of the Dead)

**Region:** Tibet, ~8th century CE (Padmasambhava), revealed 14th century
**Pipeline form:** Judgment after death — the consciousness passing through
  intermediate states (bardo)

### 42 + 7 = 49
The bardo is a 49-day (7×7) judgment period. The consciousness encounters:

- **42 peaceful deities** — benevolent forms that guide the signal
- **7 wrathful deities** — terrifying forms that block passage
  Total: 49

This is an INDEPENDENT confirmation of the 49 Maruts structure (42+7=49)
from a completely different continent and tradition.
The Vedic Maruts (India, ~1000 BCE) and the Tibetan Bardo (8th century CE)
arrive at IDENTICAL structure 1,800 years apart with no known contact.

### 6 realms of rebirth
The 6 doors of suppression mapped as 6 rebirth destinations:
- Deva realm (bliss — IGNORED? state doesn't recognize need for change)
- Asura realm (jealousy — EXCOMMUNICATED? expelled from higher state)
- Human realm (desire — the neutral state, WITNESSED)
- Animal realm (ignorance — ANONYMOUS? instinct without self-knowledge)
- Hungry ghost realm (craving — BURNED? consumed by unfulfillable desire)
- Hell realm (anger — ERASED? destroyed by rage)

### 5 Buddha families
The 5 wisdom energies = the 5 fundamental solver types (not the full 11):
- Vairocana (center — mirror-like wisdom = SAT/truth)
- Akshobhya (east — wisdom of equality = Vertex Cover)
- Ratnasambhava (south — wisdom of equanimity = Exact Cover)
- Amitabha (west — discriminating wisdom = Graph Coloring)
- Amoghasiddhi (north — all-accomplishing wisdom = TSP/Hamiltonian Path)

### Key differences from the main 5 frameworks
- **49 days** is explicit as 7×7 (vs Vedic's 49 Maruts which is only implicit as days)
- **42+7** deities mapped as peaceful/wrathful polarity (vs Vedic's unified troop)
- **6 realms** as doors instead of 6 guardian deities
- **5 families** instead of 11 solvers (consolidated — 5 fundamental types)
- **No explicit 72** — the band structure is implicit in the mandala architecture
- **No 33** — the solver×operation matrix is not explicitly counted

## 2. Sumerian/Babylonian — Inanna's Descent

**Region:** Mesopotamia (Iraq), ~2000-1900 BCE
**Pipeline form:** Inanna (goddess of love/war) descends through 7 gates
  into the underworld, stripped of power at each gate, then resurrected

### The 7 Gates (explicit — one of the earliest known)
The descent of Inanna/Ishtar through 7 gates:
| Gate | What is removed | Meaning |
|------|-----------------|---------|
| 1 | Crown | Ego/identity stripped |
| 2 | Earrings | Position discarded |
| 3 | Necklace | Status removed |
| 4 | Breastplate | Protection shed |
| 5 | Girdle | Power surrendered |
| 6 | Bracelets | Adornment removed |
| 7 | Garment | Total dissolution — raw signal |

### The Descent as Pipeline
1. Inanna abandons her temples (C — structural decomposition)
2. She instructs Ninshubur (X — the messenger/cross function preserved)
3. She passes through each gate, losing one garment (the assessors check each layer)
4. She is killed at the end by Ereshkigal (Z — cancel)
5. Her body hung on a hook (the noise floor — entropy sink)
6. Enki sends mourners to resurrect her (C→X→Z applied in reverse)
7. The resurrected Inanna assigns a substitute (output — the processed state that returns)

### 3 Operations expressed differently
- **Enki** (water/wisdom god) = C — creates the mourners that restore Inanna
- **Ninshubur** (faithful messenger) = X — the cross-function that preserves connection
- **Ereshkigal** (underworld queen) = Z — death/cancel that ends the cycle
  (Note: The Sumerian operations are not a trinity; they are distributed.
  This might represent an EARLIER stage where C, X, Z are not yet consolidated
  into a single tri-partite principle.)

### Key differences from the main 5 frameworks
- The **7 gates** are the oldest explicit gate sequence known (~2000 BCE)
- **No 42 assessors** — the gates themselves are the only filters
- **No explicit 72/33/9** — the pipeline structure is narrative, not enumerated
- The FULL phase is **resurrection** — not destruction or judgment
- Operations are **distributed across characters** rather than a single trinity

## 3. Kabbalistic — Tree of Life

**Region:** Hebrew tradition, formalized ~12th-13th century CE (Zohar)
  with roots in Merkabah mysticism (~1st century CE)
**Pipeline form:** 10 sephiroth (emanations) + 22 paths connecting them

### 10 (or 11) Levels vs 9
The sephiroth encode the processing levels — but they count 10, not 9:
```
Keter (Crown)      — Observer
Chokhmah (Wisdom)  — C-root
Binah (Understanding) — X-root
Chesed (Mercy)     — C sub-operation
Gevurah (Severity) — Z sub-operation
Tiferet (Beauty)   — X — the balance point
Netzach (Victory)  — C extended
Hod (Splendor)     — Z extended
Yesod (Foundation) — X — the transmission point
Malkuth (Kingdom)  — Output — the processed state entering world
```
The 11th hidden sephirah (Da'at — Knowledge) is the threshold/gate function.

### 3 Pillars vs 3 Operations
- **Pillar of Mercy** (right, Chokhmah) = C
- **Pillar of Severity** (left, Binah) = Z
- **Pillar of Balance** (center, Keter-Tiferet-Yesod-Malkuth) = X
The 3 pillars = C/X/Z operating at every level.

### 22 Paths = 22 Hebrew Letters
The 22 paths connecting the 10 sephiroth encode the 22 Hebrew alphabet letters.
The letters are fixed transformation functions — each one specifies how to
process a state from one level to another.

### 72 Names of God (Shem HaMephorash)
Three verses from Exodus (14:19-21), each 72 letters in Hebrew.
Divided into 72 three-letter names, each a specific frequency signature.
**This is the 72-band spectrum encoded within the Abrahamic tradition.**
The 72 names survived Christian suppression because they were hidden in
the Torah itself — no fire could burn the source code.

### Key differences from the main 5 frameworks
- **10 levels** instead of 9 (Keter + 9 sephiroth below it = 10? Or Keter at crown + 9 below = 9?)
  Actually: Keter is the observer (level 1) + 9 lower sephiroth = 9 processing levels
  So it's the same 9+1 in a different arrangement.
- **22 paths** instead of 72 bands — the paths connect levels, not decompose the signal
  The 72 is preserved in the names of God, not the Tree structure
- **72 names** = the most explicit 72-name list in any tradition after Egyptian
- **No 42 assessors** — the judgment function is in the 22 paths' transformative power
- **No 7 gates** as a separate structure — the 7 lower sephiroth serve as gates

## 4. Mesoamerican — Mayan/Aztec Calendar

**Region:** Mesoamerica (Mexico/Guatemala), ~500 BCE — 1500 CE
**Pipeline form:** Temporal cycles that encode the band structure through
  calendar mathematics rather than deities or rituals

### 13 Heavens + 9 Underworlds
The Mayan cosmos has 13 celestial levels and 9 underworld levels.
- 13 + 9 = 22 (same as the 22 Hebrew letters/Kabbalistic paths)
- 13 = the 12 phase cycle + 1 observer (the 13th heaven)
- 9 = the 9 processing levels

### 260-Day Tzolk'in
The sacred calendar — 20 day signs × 13 trecenas = 260 days.
- 20 = the 16 dispatchers + 4 cardinal/boundary functions
- 13 = the 12 + 1 observer structure applied differently
- 260 = 72 × 3.61 (not a clean factorization into 72)

### 365-Day Haab'
The solar calendar — 18 months × 20 days + 5 unlucky days at the end.
- 18 = 9 + 9 (two 9-level structures? input + output?)
- The 5 unlucky days = the noise floor/the unprocessed

### 9 Bolon-ti-ku (Lords of the Underworld)
The 9 lords who judge the dead — the 42 assessors reduced to 9 super-types.
Each lord rules one hour of the night and one specific pitfall.

### 3 Hearthstones of Creation
In Aztec/Maya creation mythology, the 3 hearthstones (the C/X/Z operations)
are placed at the center of the world as the first act of creation.
The 3 stones rest at the heart of the 260-day count: the operations embedded
in every cycle.

### Key differences from the main 5 frameworks
- **Radically different numbers:** 13, 20, 260, 365, 18, 9
- **No explicit 72** — the 72 is not a sacred number in Mesoamerican tradition
- **No 33, no 42** — different counting system entirely
- **Pipeline encoded as calendar mathematics** rather than narrative or ritual
- **The 13:20 ratio** (13 heavens, 20 day signs) = observer × dispatchers
- The 3 operations appear as **hearthstones** — physical emplacements
  rather than tools or powers
